# Dynamic FFN group oracle

This diagnostic asks whether per-token selection can remove enough Qwen3.6-27B
FFN channel groups to justify a sparse gfx11 backend. It is not a serving path
and does not currently reduce GEMM work.

After the retained fused SwiGLU producer creates the rotated group128 Q8 down
input, one GPU workgroup per token reconstructs the L2 energy of each
256-channel group, keeps the top groups, and zeroes the rest. Gate/up and the
dense down GEMM still execute in full. The experiment therefore measures an
optimistic quality boundary for a future predictor and sparse gate/up/down
backend, not a speedup.

## W7900 results

Configuration: Qwen3.6-27B MQ4, gfx1100 W7900, asym3 KV, real text from
`docs/testINPUT.md`, 4 x 512-token chunks, stride 4, 256 paired scored
positions. The baseline and candidates score identical token positions.

| Groups kept | FFN groups retained | PPL | PPL ratio | Mean NLL delta | 95% CI | Top-1 agreement | Top-8 overlap |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 68 | 100.0% | 8.5672 | 1.0000 | 0 | - | 100.00% | 8.00/8 |
| 60 | 88.2% | 8.6542 | 1.0102 | +0.0101 | [-0.0102, +0.0304] | 94.92% | 7.54/8 |
| 51 | 75.0% | 8.8758 | 1.0360 | +0.0354 | [-0.0068, +0.0776] | 91.80% | 7.22/8 |
| 41 | 60.3% | 9.3596 | 1.0925 | +0.0885 | [+0.0135, +0.1634] | 90.23% | 6.88/8 |

A second out-of-domain run used the fixed WikiText2 slice at
`benchmarks/quality-baselines/slice/wikitext2-1024s-2048ctx.txt`, with 4 x
2048-token chunks, stride 8, and 512 paired positions:

| Groups kept | FFN groups retained | PPL | PPL ratio | Mean NLL delta | 95% CI | Top-1 agreement | Top-8 overlap |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 68 | 100.0% | 12.8614 | 1.0000 | 0 | - | 100.00% | 8.00/8 |
| 60 | 88.2% | 13.7510 | 1.0692 | +0.0669 | [-0.0048, +0.1386] | 89.26% | 6.89/8 |
| 41 | 60.3% | 19.6511 | 1.5279 | +0.4239 | [+0.2680, +0.5798] | 71.48% | 5.64/8 |

The 41-group point approximates the 40% FFN work reduction needed for the
1.5k tok/s target. It fails both corpora, with a statistically significant
9.25% PPL increase on the short document and a 52.79% increase on WikiText2.
It is rejected. Keeping 60 groups was unresolved in both small samples, but
its out-of-domain PPL increase of 6.92% and top-1 agreement of 89.26% reject it
as a transparent production optimization. Even if that quality loss were
accepted as a separate model profile, the ideal affected wall share remains
below 10% and cannot close the 1.12k-to-1.5k gap by itself.

The route is closed for a transparent backend. Future work would require
retraining or distillation, a pre-gate/up predictor, a real sparse MQ4
primitive, and an explicit approximate-model contract. The fixed/static and
dynamic experiments use different quality harnesses, so their absolute PPL
deltas must not be ranked directly.

Run the short-document sweep with:

```bash
GPU_ID=1 experiments/gfx11-mq4-v2/dynamic-ffn-oracle/run_quality_sweep.sh
```

Reproduce the WikiText2 configuration by overriding the corpus and window:

```bash
GPU_ID=1 \
CORPUS=benchmarks/quality-baselines/slice/wikitext2-1024s-2048ctx.txt \
CTX=2048 CHUNKS=4 STRIDE=8 \
OUT_DIR=/tmp/ffn-oracle-wikitext \
experiments/gfx11-mq4-v2/dynamic-ffn-oracle/run_quality_sweep.sh
```

The script emits raw binary records, logs, and paired comparison text. The
checked-in summary tables are `results/w7900-20260811/quality.tsv` and
`wikitext-quality.tsv`; they are derived from those raw outputs rather than
generated by the script.
